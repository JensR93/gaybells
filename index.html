About

Roecker Digital Services is a one-person design studio in Germany. I already create graphics and videos for my own websites and YouTube channels. This series turns workout jargon into acronyms and wordplay, set in bold type and bright, opaque colors made for direct-to-garment printing on dark shirts. Every phrase is checked against the USPTO, EUIPO and DPMA trademark registers before it goes online. Fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License.
